How to use Fuwa.NG

Simple, step-by-step guides to the three services our users ask about most: the NIN Suite, the BVN Suite, and the VTU Hub. Every service on Fuwa.NG is paid from your wallet, so start with the three steps below.

Getting Started

Do these three things once, and every service below will work for you.

1. Create your account and log in

  1. Register with your full name, a valid email address and your phone number.
  2. Log in to your dashboard. Your wallet balance is shown on the dashboard and inside each service.

2. Fund your wallet

  1. From your dashboard, open Fund Wallet.
  2. Pay with your card, or transfer to the dedicated virtual account number shown for you.
  3. Your wallet is credited automatically once the payment is confirmed. Every service fee is deducted from this wallet — the exact fee is always displayed before you confirm a transaction.

3. Complete your own KYC verification

For your protection, purchases are only enabled after you have verified your own identity on your account (your NIN or BVN, under the account verification section). This one-time step unlocks the NIN Suite, BVN Suite and VTU Hub checkouts.

How to verify a NIN

  1. Open the NIN Suite and choose NIN Verification (or go straight to fuwa.ng/services/nin).
  2. Enter the 11-digit NIN exactly as issued by NIMC.
  3. Confirm the fee shown on the page and submit. The fee is deducted from your wallet only for the service you selected.
  4. The verified record — names, date of birth, phone number and photo where available — is displayed immediately and saved to your history, so you can reopen it any time without paying again.

How to print a NIN slip

  1. Verify the NIN first (steps above), or open a previous verification from your history.
  2. Choose the slip type: Standard, Premium, Regular or vNIN.
  3. Download the generated slip as a PDF and print it. Slips from your history can be re-downloaded from the same record.

“Record Not Found”? Use NIN Validation

If a bank or SIM point says your NIN record cannot be found, the record usually needs validation on the NIMC side. Open NIN Validation, submit the NIN, and allow the processing time shown on the page. You can check the status of a validation request from the same page — do not submit the same NIN twice while one request is still processing.

Other NIN services, in brief

  • Personalization: if all you have is the enrolment tracking ID, use Personalization to generate the active NIN and slip from it.
  • IPE Clearance: for records stuck with an Internal Processing Error, submit the clearance request and track it to completion.
  • Face Verification: open Face Verification, enter the NIN and take a clear selfie in good light when prompted — the system matches your face against the NIMC photo.
  • NIN Modification: to correct details on a record, use the NIN Modification page, accept the consent notice, and submit the correct details. Modification requests are processed and their status can be tracked.
Agents: verified enrolment agents get wholesale rates on the NIN Suite — an automatic 15% discount on verification and slip fees — with the same wallet shared across the agent and user dashboards.

BVN Suite Bank Verification

Verify a Bank Verification Number against the banking record, or retrieve a BVN the owner has lost or forgotten. Open it here: fuwa.ng/services/bvn

How to verify a BVN

  1. Open the BVN Suite and stay on the BVN Lookup tab.
  2. Enter the 11-digit BVN, the account owner’s first name and last name, and the date of birth — exactly as they appear on the bank account. The details must match the bank record for the check to succeed.
  3. Choose your verification level:
    • Essential Data — the core identity details on the BVN record.
    • Premium + Image — the full record including the enrolment photo.
  4. Confirm the fee shown and click Run BVN Check. The result appears immediately and is saved under BVN Verification History on the same page, where you can also download it as a PDF.

Lost or forgotten BVN? Use BVN Retrieval

If the account owner no longer knows their BVN, you can request a retrieval instead of a lookup:

  1. On the BVN page, go to the BVN Retrieval section.
  2. Enter the phone number linked to the BVN and the account owner’s full name. Adding the date of birth (optional) speeds up matching.
  3. Confirm the fee and submit the request.
  4. Most requests resolve within 24 hours, every day including weekends. Track yours under My Retrieval Requests and use Check Status to refresh it.
  5. When completed, the retrieved BVN is shown on the request and saved to your vault.
Refund guarantee: if a retrieval request ends with the BVN not found, the fee is refunded to your wallet automatically — the request will show as Refunded.

The general way every VTU purchase works

  1. Make sure your wallet is funded — the VTU Hub shows your available balance at the top.
  2. Pick the service (for example Data Bundles).
  3. Enter the beneficiary details (phone number, smartcard/IUC number, or meter number) and choose the plan or amount. For cable and electricity, the system validates the number first and shows the customer name — confirm it is the right person before paying.
  4. Confirm the total and submit. Delivery is automated, usually within seconds.
  5. Your receipt — including tokens and transaction references — appears immediately in Recent Transactions on the hub and in your full transaction history.

Airtime

  1. Open Airtime Top-up, choose the network, enter the phone number and the amount, and confirm.

Data bundles

  1. Open Data Bundles, choose the network and the beneficiary phone number.
  2. Select the plan type (SME, corporate or gifting) and the exact bundle — size, validity and price are shown on each plan — then confirm.

Cable TV (DStv, GOtv, StarTimes)

  1. Open Cable TV, choose the provider and enter the smartcard / IUC number.
  2. Wait for validation to display the customer name and current bouquet, choose the package, and confirm payment.

Electricity (prepaid & postpaid)

  1. Open Electricity Bills, choose your disco and meter type, and enter the meter number.
  2. Confirm the customer name and address shown after validation, enter the amount, and pay.
  3. For prepaid meters, your token is displayed on the confirmation and saved in your transaction history — copy it before leaving the page, and retrieve it from history any time.

Education PINs (WAEC, NECO, NABTEB, JAMB)

  1. From the VTU Hub, open the exam you need: WAEC (result checker or registration PIN), NECO, NABTEB or JAMB ePIN (UTME / Direct Entry).
  2. Choose the quantity, confirm the total, and pay from your wallet. PINs are delivered instantly on screen and kept in your transaction history.
Keep your reference: every VTU order has an order reference in your history. If a token or PIN ever seems delayed, quote that reference when you contact support — it lets the team re-query the exact transaction in seconds.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
A service says my KYC must be completed firstComplete the one-time identity verification on your own account (NIN or BVN under account verification), then return to the service and try again.
My wallet was debited but the service shows as processingWait a few minutes and refresh your transaction history — automated deliveries can queue briefly. If it is still unresolved, contact support with the transaction reference.
NIN verification says “Record Not Found”The record needs validation. Use NIN Validation instead of repeating the verification, and check its status from the validation page.
BVN check failed even though the BVN is correctThe name and date of birth you entered must match the bank record exactly. Correct them and run the check again — or use BVN Retrieval if the owner’s details are uncertain.
I paid for electricity but cannot find my tokenOpen your transaction history — the token is saved on the transaction. It is also shown on the purchase confirmation screen.
My wallet funding has not reflectedConfirm the payment completed with your bank or card provider and keep the payment reference. Funding is credited automatically once payment is confirmed; if it remains uncredited, contact support with the reference.
